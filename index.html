Surakshit Nau

सुरक्षित नौ Maternal registry prototype for rural care teams: an ASHA's phone and a PHC doctor's dashboard, working on the same records.
Prototype · sample data · all names and IDs are fictional
  1. Register a mother offline. On the phone, tap Online to go offline, then register a mother or log a check-up. It saves on the phone.
  2. Go back online to sync. Tap Offline again. Waiting records reach the PHC dashboard and are highlighted there.
  3. Follow up from the PHC. Open any mother on the dashboard to record a referral, add the care team's high-risk mark, or preview an SMS reminder in Hindi.
ASHA mobile app
PHC doctor dashboard

Surakshit Nau records and tracks care. It does not diagnose, score risk, interpret results or recommend treatment. Health values are stored exactly as entered. A high-risk mark is added only by a doctor or ANM, with their own note. The check-up schedule follows India's four-visit antenatal care schedule (ANC 1 by 12 weeks, ANC 2 at 14–26, ANC 3 at 28–34, ANC 4 from 36 weeks). No SMS is sent from this prototype.
