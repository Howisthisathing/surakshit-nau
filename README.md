# Surakshit Nau (सुरक्षित नौ)

**Keeping every mother safe, all nine months.**

Surakshit Nau is a unified digital maternal registry for rural India. It helps PHC doctors, ANMs and ASHA workers track every pregnancy, so no follow-up is missed.

- **Hackathon track:** Maternal & Women's Health
- **Use case:** Patient Registry & Population Health
- **Primary users:** Doctors and care teams at Primary Health Centres (PHCs)

## Live prototype

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages link for this repository.

The prototype shows two views working on the same records:

1. **ASHA mobile app:** register pregnant women and log check-ups in English or Hindi. It works offline and syncs when back online.
2. **PHC doctor dashboard:** village-wise lists of missed and upcoming check-ups, pending referrals, and SMS reminder previews in Hindi and English.

### Try it

1. On the phone, tap **Online** to go offline, then register a mother or log a check-up.
2. Tap **Offline** to go back online. Waiting records appear on the dashboard, highlighted.
3. On the dashboard, click **Open** on any mother to record a referral, add the care team's high-risk mark, or preview an SMS reminder.

## Scope

Surakshit Nau records and tracks care. It does **not** diagnose, score risk, interpret medical data or recommend treatment.

- Health values are stored exactly as entered.
- A high-risk mark can only be added by a doctor or ANM, with their own note.
- The check-up schedule follows India's four-visit antenatal care schedule: ANC 1 by 12 weeks, ANC 2 at 14–26 weeks, ANC 3 at 28–34 weeks, and ANC 4 from 36 weeks.



## Data notice

All names, ABHA numbers, phone numbers and records in the prototype are fictional sample data. No SMS is sent.
